# Voter Guide — MOBA 2017 Elections

## 1. Your login details

When your membership is approved, you receive an email titled **“Your MOBA 2017 election login details”** with
your email address and a **temporary password**. Check your spam folder if you cannot find it.

## 2. First sign-in

1. Open the election website and choose **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and the temporary password.
3. Choose your own password (at least 10 characters; do not use your name or email). You are then taken to your
   dashboard.

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page; a link valid for 30 minutes is emailed
to you.

## 3. Before voting

On your dashboard open the election to see:
* when voting opens and closes (Ghana time),
* the voting instructions and rules,
* every candidate's photo, biography and manifesto.

## 4. Voting

1. While voting is open, choose **Vote now** (or **Go to the ballot**).
2. For each position pick one candidate — or **I abstain** where allowed.
3. Choose **Review my choices**, check them, then **Submit my vote** and confirm.
4. You will see **“Your vote has been recorded”** and a **receipt code**. Write it down if you wish — it is shown
   only once and is not emailed.

Your ballot **cannot be changed** after submission. You can vote only once.

## 5. Your vote is secret

The system records *that* you voted separately from *what* you chose. Your choices are stored without your name,
email or the time of voting. Administrators and the Electoral Committee can see who has voted, but **nobody can
see how you voted**. The confirmation email only says that you took part.

## 6. If something goes wrong

* **Lost connection after submitting?** Sign in again. Your dashboard shows “You voted on …” if your ballot was
  recorded. If it says you have not voted, you can vote.
* **“Voting has not started” / “Voting has ended”** — the server clock decides; check the times on the election page.
* **Account locked** after several wrong passwords — wait 15 minutes or reset your password.
* Still stuck? See **Help & support** on the website for contact details.

## 7. Checking your receipt

Go to **Verify a receipt**, enter your code, and the site confirms whether your ballot is stored and counted.
